# Honghui Chen (Sage Chan)

PhD student in **Psychiatry** at **The University of Edinburgh**, based at the **Centre for Clinical Brain Sciences (CCBS)**, School of Neurological and Cardiovascular Sciences, College of Medicine and Veterinary Medicine.

**Supervisor:** [Dr Alex Kwong](https://edwebprofiles.ed.ac.uk/node/113985)

My research sits at the intersection of **AI for Psychology**, **NeuroAI**, and **Digital Mental Health**. I use machine learning and large language models to develop and evaluate psychological assessment and mental-health research tools.

## Research interests

- AI for psychology
- Mental health data science
- Large language models and multi-agent systems
- Digital mental health


## Selected work

- **PsychoGAT:** psychological measurement through interactive-fiction games with LLM agents — [ACL 2024](https://doi.org/10.18653/v1/2024.acl-long)
- **Adolescent self-injury risk networks:** machine learning and network analysis — [Translational Psychiatry, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41398-025-03511-3)
- **Life-satisfaction trajectories in older adults:** machine-learning predictors — [Applied Psychology: Health and Well-Being, 2024](https://doi.org/10.1111/aphw.12579)
